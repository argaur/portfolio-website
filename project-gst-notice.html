One number,
no reason.

01 · Problem

A GST DRC-01C notice shows the gap between the input tax credit a taxpayer claimed and what their suppliers reported. The portal gives the gap and a form to reply. It does not show which invoice or supplier record caused it.

Most recipients have no accountant or reconciliation tool on hand when the notice arrives. The reply window is fixed, and a wrong reply can block a later filing.

Between the notice
and the reply.

02 · Flow
  1. Show the notice and the total difference.
  2. Reconcile row by row in plain code. Each row is labelled: exact match, missing in 2B, possible duplicate, or later-period match.
  3. Explain the findings. The model only explains what the code has already checked, and cites the rows.
  4. Draft a review-only reply the taxpayer can copy, print or discard.

A second round added a risk screen for supplier-filed bills the taxpayer must act on, and a question box in Hindi or English that answers by citing real row IDs.

What came
of it.

05 · Result

The prototype shipped and is live. It placed in the Top 250. It runs on synthetic data. No real taxpayer has used it, and I make no claim about accuracy on real notices.

The design choice I would keep: code decides, the model explains. That kept the numbers checkable and kept the model away from anything it could invent.
