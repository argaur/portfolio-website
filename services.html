Services

Test one product idea or fix one messy workflow.

I help founders and small B2B teams test one product idea or fix one messy workflow. You get a working prototype or system, with clear limits and a handover your team can own.

I start with the process, not the software. Together, we identify what is worth fixing, agree on a small scope and build something your team can understand and own. AI is an option when it solves a real problem, not a requirement.

Two ways to start: Workflow automation · AI product sprint

Start with a workflow diagnostic

Know what to automate before paying to build it.

For teams that know a process is slow or unreliable, but are not sure where to start.

Over 3 to 5 working days, I review one workflow, the tools it touches and the handoffs that cause delays. You get:

  • A current-process map and the main failure points.
  • A prioritised set of changes, including what should stay manual.
  • A proposed workflow, tool choices and acceptance criteria.
  • A written implementation scope with an indicative cost and timeline.
  • A walkthrough so you can decide what to do next.

Final scope, timeline and price are agreed before work starts.

The diagnostic is a paid, standalone engagement. It does not include production changes, and you do not have to hire me for the build.

One workflow sprint

Build the first useful workflow, without taking on a full transformation project.

For teams with a clear process and an owner who can review the work.

In roughly two weeks, I build one agreed workflow using Airtable and Make across up to 2 or 3 systems. Examples include enquiry-to-CRM intake, client onboarding, approval tracking, or an internal operations tracker.

The sprint includes:

  • A scoped workflow and agreed acceptance criteria.
  • Airtable setup and Make automations needed for that workflow.
  • Testing of the agreed happy path and error cases.
  • Up to two review rounds.
  • Documentation and a handover walkthrough.
  • 14 days of defect support for the delivered scope after acceptance.

Final scope, timeline and price are agreed before work starts.

Your software subscriptions, API usage and other third-party costs are separate. Large data migrations, custom hosting, security certification, extra workflows and ongoing or 24/7 support are not included. Changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.

AI product sprint

Test one product idea with a working prototype.

For founders and small B2B teams with one product question that a working demo can answer.

In about three weeks, I run discovery, write a short PRD and build one core user path. I test it against an agreed set of cases and tell you what failed. You get the demo, the test results, a known-limits note, the code and a handover walkthrough.

The first step is a free short call. I then send a written scope, and no work starts until you agree to it.

Scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts.

A prototype is not a production launch. Production hardening, security review and hosting at scale are a separate quote. Your API, hosting and subscription costs are separate.

How we work

Four steps, agreed up front.

  1. Describe the workflow and where it breaks.
  2. Start with a diagnostic for a workflow, or a short call and a written scope for a prototype.
  3. Review working progress and test against agreed criteria.
  4. Take ownership with documentation, a walkthrough and defined support.

I work remotely from Jaipur. We agree on meeting overlap and review dates before starting.
